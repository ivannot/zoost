Privacy policy

Zoost — Chrome extension · Last updated: 3 August 2026

The short version

Zoost collects nothing. It has no server, no account, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. Your data moves between your browser, the Zoho CRM instance you are already signed in to, and a folder on your own disk. The single exception is the optional AI assistant, which sends what you ask it about to the AI provider you configure with your own API key. Nothing is ever sent to the developer.

1. Who is responsible

Zoost is developed and published by Ivan Notaristefano, an individual developer, as an independent, unofficial tool. Contact: ivan@zoost.it.

Zoost is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or supported by Zoho Corporation. Data held inside your Zoho CRM remains governed by your own agreement with Zoho and by your organisation's policies; this document covers only what the Zoost extension does with it.

2. What the developer collects

Nothing. The extension contains no analytics, no telemetry, no error reporting, no advertising identifiers, no cookies and no remotely hosted code. There is no backend service operated by the developer, so there is no place for your data to arrive.

The developer therefore does not sell, share, transfer or otherwise process your personal data, because it never receives any.

3. What is stored, and where

Everything Zoost stores lives on the computer you are using.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. You can erase all of it at any time by removing the extension and deleting the working folder. Removing the extension deletes the browser-side storage; the local folder is yours and is left untouched.

4. Where data travels

4.1 Your Zoho CRM

Zoost calls the Zoho CRM API of the instance open in your tab, using the session you are already signed in with. It sends no credentials of its own and creates none. It operates strictly within your existing Zoho permissions: it can read exactly what your Zoho user can read, and nothing more.

Zoost reads metadata and function source. It does not read, download or export your CRM records — no contacts, no deals, no customer data.

4.2 The AI provider — only if you enable it

Read this before enabling the assistant

The AI assistant is off by default and requires you to enter your own API key. Once enabled, the content it needs to answer you — which includes Deluge source code, module schemas, workflow definitions and your questions — is sent directly from your browser to the provider you selected: Anthropic or OpenAI. No other destination is possible: the extension is granted network access to those two origins and to your Zoho CRM, and to nothing else.

That provider then processes the data under its own terms and privacy policy, not this one. The developer of Zoost is not a party to that exchange, cannot see it and has no control over it. If your organisation restricts sending source code or business logic to third-party AI services, do not enable this feature.

Your API key is stored only in chrome.storage.local on your machine and is sent only to the provider it belongs to.

4.3 Exports

Export files are written to your working folder and go nowhere on their own. Before each export, Zoost asks which sections may be included; source code is opt-in and is flagged when selected. What happens to a file after you create it is your responsibility and that of the organisation whose data it describes.

5. Permissions, and why each one exists

  • sidePanel — the extension's interface is a Chrome side panel.
  • storage — to keep your settings, as described above.
  • scripting — to inject the bridge script that talks to the Zoho CRM API using your session, and the hook that notices when you save a function so the local copy can follow.
  • tabs — to detect which Zoho CRM instance and org the active tab belongs to, so that a production workspace can never be synced against a sandbox by mistake.
  • Host access to Zoho CRM domains (crm.zoho.*, crmsandbox.zoho.*, one.zoho.*) — the only sites the extension runs on. It is inert everywhere else.
  • Host access to api.anthropic.com and api.openai.com — the only two AI destinations the extension can reach, used solely by the optional AI assistant and only after you configure it.

Zoost requests no access to your browsing history, bookmarks, downloads, saved passwords or any site other than those listed above.

6. Legal basis and your rights

Because the developer receives no personal data, there is no processing to consent to, object to, or request access to. Data protection rights in respect of the CRM data itself are exercised against the controller of that data — your organisation — and, where relevant, against the AI provider you chose, under their respective policies.

7. Children

Zoost is a developer and administration tool for business software. It is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 16.

8. This website

zoost.it is a static site. It sets no cookies, embeds no trackers, loads no third-party fonts, scripts or analytics, and does not profile visitors. Your hosting provider may keep standard server access logs; those are outside the scope of this policy.

9. Changes

If a future version of Zoost changes what data is handled or where it travels, this policy will be updated and the date at the top revised before that version is published. Material changes will be noted in the extension's release notes.
