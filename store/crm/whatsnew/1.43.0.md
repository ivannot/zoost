**The relationship diagram was rebuilt to work on a real org.** It used to draw a small picture in
the middle of a large empty window and then give up quietly on anything big: at a few hundred boxes
it left hundreds of them covering each other and said nothing about it. The layout now sizes itself
to what it is actually drawing, so a focused diagram fills the window instead of sitting in the
middle of it, and the pass that keeps boxes apart was made to work hardest exactly where there are
most boxes to separate - which is the opposite of what it did before.

**How much it will draw is a setting, and the number beside the tab tells you where you stand.** The
ceiling is **800** boxes by default, because a real org came in at 725 and 400 would have refused it,
and you can raise it in Settings - which states what raising it buys you (more of the graph, not a
better picture of it) and what each size costs in seconds. Past **80** boxes the count turns amber
and warns you the drawing will be crowded, and then draws it anyway. That is your call to make.

**The whole-org view is reachable again.** A readability limit had been written as a wall, and an org
with more than eighty items in one category could never get under it by any combination of filters -
so the limit did not make the whole view worse, it removed it. Reported, and right.

**Arrange the diagram yourself, and keep the arrangement.** Drag a box where it belongs: the
automatic layout is a starting point, and past eighty boxes it crowds whatever it does - you are the
one who knows which two have to sit side by side. A box you drag rises above the others and stays
there, and the drop tells you how many boxes it now covers. Change a filter and what you moved keeps
its place; only the boxes new to the drawing are placed for you, and they make room **around** what
you built rather than pushing it aside. Two boxes you overlapped yourself stay overlapped, because
you put them there.

And it no longer dies with the window. Under the diagram's new **File** button: **Save PDF** as
before, plus **Save arrangement** and **Load arrangement**. An arrangement is a plain file you name
yourself, so one diagram can have several readings - one arranged for a presentation, one for
chasing a problem - and you can hand one to a colleague or keep it beside the mirror. Nothing is
restored automatically and there is no list of saved arrangements to manage: they are files, in your
folders, under your names.

Load one onto an org the mirror has moved on and **the diagram of today wins, with every loss
counted**: a box that is gone drops its position, a box that is new is placed by the layout around
what you kept, and a box that was renamed loses its position rather than inheriting a stranger's - a
box sitting where a different one used to sit would misstate the shape you built. The status line
says what happened in numbers, including folded branches that no longer apply, and if everything
still matches while the relations have changed, it says that too.

**Take a branch off the drawing.** Where an arc meets a box there is now a mark that removes the box
at the other end, together with everything that can no longer be reached without it - so anything
reachable another way stays. Before you press, the tooltip names what will go and counts it, each
name in the colour its box wears, and the ones currently on screen are outlined at the same moment.
A **+** in the same place brings it all back, and the mark's own colours say which way the relation
runs and what is at the other end of it. It hides rather than laying the diagram out again, so it
composes with an arrangement instead of destroying it.

**Getting around it.** Double-click empty canvas to zoom in on that point, which ends up in the
middle of the window; **Fit** frames the drawing - which it did not do once you had arranged
anything, because it measured the distance from the corner rather than the drawing itself. Dragging
the sheet to pan no longer leaves every label it passed over highlighted.

**Nothing reaches Zoho while the tab is a different org.** Pull was already refused; everything else
that reads from Zoho now refuses too, at the moment it would act and again at the one door out - so
a control that looks enabled, or one somebody re-enables, still cannot act on the wrong org. The bar
that says the two differ used to claim that everything was disabled, which was never true: the
mirror on disk stays readable, and it now says so. The overlay that curtained off the list is gone
with it.

**When a read from Zoho fails, the panel says a fix may already be released** - beside the status
line, where you are actually looking when something breaks, rather than only on the website.

**Also:** the group headers in **Actions** fold like every other group; an empty list in the sample
workspace no longer tells you to press a button that is refused for a sample, and says what would
fill it; switching a category off used to change the chip and leave the diagram exactly as it was;
the Settings page no longer shows a slider that was retired two versions earlier, and its ceiling
value no longer wraps onto two lines; the message shown when the diagram refuses to draw quoted the
wrong default; folding a branch no longer lets the frame keep sizing itself for boxes nobody can
see; arcs arriving at the same side of a box land apart instead of converging on one point, and a
box grows when it needs the room for them; and the toolbar icon is drawn at the size Chrome asks for
rather than scaled down from a larger one.
