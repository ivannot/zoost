**What a rule actually fires: the new Actions tab.** A workflow rule almost never does the work
itself - it points at an email notification, a field update, a task or a webhook, each of which
exists on its own in Zoho CRM and gets reused across rules. Those four are now a list of their own,
with a **Kind** filter, and each row carries the thing you cannot see in Zoho: **how many rules fire
it**, read from the rules already on your disk. Open one and it shows what it is made of - the
template a notification uses and the address it goes out as, the field an update writes and the value
it writes, a task's subject, due date, owner and reminder, a webhook's method and URL.

**About half of them turn out to be attached to nothing.** Measured on one real org: 85 notifications
of 200, 50 field updates of 97, 27 tasks of 56. The filter is **Attached to nothing**, and the same
list appears in the health audit. These are candidates, exactly as an orphan function is - Zoho
answers for the rules it knows about, and something fired from elsewhere would look identical.

**The first diagram is now called Wiring, and it holds the whole org.** It used to draw functions
calling functions. It draws everything that runs and the modules it touches: workflows, schedules and
connections are nodes in their own right, so you can see what starts a piece of code and what it
reaches on the way out. A connection nothing uses is visible at a glance.

**Two things it does not read, and one you decide.** Never the content of an email template - Zoho is
where you read that - and never who the recipients are, only how many. The sender's address *is*
captured, because "which address does this go out as" is the question the list exists to answer.
Whether that address leaves your machine is yours to set, separately, in the export dialog and in
Settings; both are off by default.

**Fixes, all of them reported.** Switching workspace left the Health view showing the previous org.
The Connections status dot showed the connection's own status instead of whether your copy was
current. The chat's footer was painted over its textarea and Send button. A sixth tab pushed the
segment row onto two lines. The diagram window never picked up the name you gave a workspace. A key
column holding a sentence drew over the answer beside it. Actions rows had columns that shifted from
row to row and no way to sort them. A field update that clears a field said nothing at all, and a
task's contents were read from Zoho's summary sentence rather than from its own fields.
