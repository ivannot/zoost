# Zoost CRM 2.0.0

**Zoost is a window of its own.** The side panel is gone. Clicking the toolbar icon opens Zoost as a
window you can resize, move and put on a second monitor, with Zoho on the first - which is how this
product is actually used, and what a side panel could never do: Chrome caps one at two thirds of the
browser window it is attached to, so the old ceiling was structural rather than a matter of layout.
Where you put the window and how big you made it is remembered and used the next time you open it.

**Everything is in that one window now.** The diagram and the settings used to open as separate
browser windows; they are views of the workbench, shown over it and closed with the same control in
the same corner. Nothing else on your screen moves when you open one.

**The panel uses the width it is given.** Past about 720px the list and the detail sit side by side
instead of stacked, and the find box and the filters share one band rather than taking a row each -
on a wide window that is a whole group more of the list on screen. Narrow, nothing has changed: the
layout you have today is the layout you keep. The divider is draggable in both arrangements, and the
two sizes are remembered separately, because how tall you want the code in a narrow panel is not how
wide you want it in a broad one.

**Zoost reads your mirror whether or not a Zoho tab is in front of you.** A mirror is local files -
the lists, the search, the diagrams, the exports and the assistant need no tab at all - and they are
no longer covered by the screen that asks you to open one. What needs Zoho still says so: pulls and
the links into Zoho CRM switch off, and the panel tells you why.

**It finds the right tab when several are open.** Production and a sandbox side by side is the
ordinary arrangement. Zoost asks the candidates which org they are on and uses the one that matches
the workspace you have open, instead of the first tab it happened to find; a tab that has gone quiet
- which is what an extension reload does to pages that were already open - is revived once before
being judged. And the mismatch warning is about the tab you are looking at. Standing somewhere that
has nothing to do with Zoho no longer raises an alarm about a tab you cannot see.

**The context bar says one thing when there is one thing to say.** It carried ten facts, and when the
tab and the workspace agreed it said the same thing twice. The tab half is drawn only when it differs,
the relation leads - a red mark before, a green tick after - and the eleven-digit org ids moved into
the tooltip, where a thing you verify belongs.

**A mismatch closes nothing that is already on disk.** Opening a workspace whose org does not match
the tab used to shut what you were reading; it now says so and leaves your mirror open.

**«Go to» opens a tab of its own.** Every control that takes you into Zoho - the item you are
looking at, the functions list, the module's records - used to navigate the Zoho tab you already
had. That was right while Zoost lived inside the browser window: the tab beside the panel was the
one you were working in. It is not right from a window of its own, where the tab it takes over is
one of your others - press it on a function you are half-way through editing in Zoho's editor and
the edit is gone. A page you already have open is brought forward instead of opened twice, and
Ctrl-click and Shift-click still mean what they mean everywhere. **And if you work inside Zoho One
or Zoho CRM Plus, the new tab keeps the suite around it**: the address is rebuilt on the shell you are
standing in, read off your own tab rather than assumed, so you land where you are used to landing.

**A keyboard shortcut, because the side panel's own went with it:** **Alt+Shift+Z** opens Zoost
(**Ctrl+Shift+Z** on a Mac). Chrome lets you change it at `chrome://extensions/shortcuts`, and it is
the only shortcut this extension declares.

Nothing on disk changes and nothing about what leaves your machine changes. Your mirror, your
settings and your API key are read exactly as they were; there is no migration and nothing to redo.
"Major" here means Zoost looks different, not that something you relied on has gone.
