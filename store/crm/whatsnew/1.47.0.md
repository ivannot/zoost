**Zoho One and Zoho CRM Plus.** If you reach your Zoho CRM through `one.zoho.<dc>` or `crmplus.zoho.<dc>`,
Zoost now works there. Those addresses put Zoho CRM inside a frame, and the panel had no way to find
it - it opened and did nothing, with no explanation. It locates Zoho CRM by asking each frame and
keeping the one that answers, so a suite shell, a sandbox and a plain Zoho CRM all behave the same.
Nine data centres, plus the ones already covered.

**"Open in Zoho" goes to the right place inside a suite.** It used to move the whole tab, which is
the shell rather than Zoho CRM.

**The HTML report.** It shares its frame with the Zoho Analytics report now: a heading band
that stays with you, a filter box, a table of contents, and every link landing on its section
instead of a few lines under the band. Two fixes to it as well - an export with source ticked could
fail outright, and so could an export from a fresh panel.

**Empty states say the one thing that is in the way.** A stored folder loses its permission between
sessions; the panel now says so, and a click anywhere in it restores the folder rather than sending
you round the whole setup again.

**The assistant** no longer answers with an empty message when the model spends its whole budget
thinking, and says when a reply was cut short instead of presenting half an answer as a whole one.

Plus a long list of smaller corrections across the panel, the diagrams, the health audit and both
reports - most of them found by driving the product rather than reading it.
