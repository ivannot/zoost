# Zoost CRM 1.53.0

**A pull redraws what you have open.** If the mirror gains something a module did not have before -
its pipelines, a column, a related list - it appears while you are looking at it, instead of only
after you select another module and come back. Where you were in the page, and the groups you had
collapsed in the list, stay where you left them.

**Open it on a Zoho Analytics tab and it says so.** It used to refuse with "Not on a Zoho tab", which
was wrong twice over: the tab *was* Zoho, and the message did not say which product was wanted. It
now names the tab you are on, says that Zoost Analytics reads it, and either tells you to click that
extension's icon in your toolbar or offers you its listing - whichever is true, because it asks the
other extension whether it is there. Inside Zoho One or Zoho CRM Plus, which can host either
product, it keeps the plain message rather than guess which one you meant.

**That screen is three groups instead of one list.** What to do when the tab belongs to the other
product, the pair that takes you to Zoho CRM - the data-centre list exists only for the button beside
it - and the sample workspace at the foot. Each bordered in the colour of the product it is about.

**A sample workspace is never shown as a live org.** Leaving a Zoho tab with the sample open used to
flip the workspace label to a production-looking binding, invented data included. It says what it is.

One caveat, true of this release only. The half that answers the question ships *with* this
version, on both products - so until Chrome has updated them both, the one that has not moved
yet stays silent and this panel will offer you its listing. That is a link, never a claim that
you lack it: nothing can tell an extension that is absent from one that is a version behind,
and the sentence beside the link says so. Once both have updated the question is answered
properly, and no answer is kept for more than thirty seconds, so it corrects itself without
you doing anything.

Nothing about what leaves your machine has changed. The one message this version adds goes to the
other Zoost extension inside your own browser, is one word long, and never leaves the machine;
the privacy page on zoost.it describes it.
