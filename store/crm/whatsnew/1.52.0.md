# Zoost for Zoho CRM 1.52.0

**Every control answers the keyboard.** Sixteen glyph buttons, the settings and About dialogs, the
assistant's controls and the diagram window's chips were clickable and nothing else; they are real
buttons now, Escape closes whatever is on top, and the focus ring is drawn where the browser left it
invisible.

**For each field, the workflow rules it makes fire.** The Fields table has a Workflows column that
counts the rules a field starts, the ones whose conditions check it, and the ones whose field update
writes it - sortable, and the number opens them grouped by what they do, one link per rule. It says
how many rules it could not read rather than pretending the count is complete.

**A module's pipelines**, the ladders its records climb: stages, outcomes, probabilities and
forecast, on screen and in both reports.

**Blueprints**, the processes records walk: the list an org holds, per module, with the states and
what each transition does - the field an update writes, the function it calls.

**Pull list, beside Pull.** Functions, Modules, Workflows and Actions each read their index alone in
seconds, or the full detail when you ask for it; a pull that came up short is marked partial and
counted rather than filed as complete.

**The report you export.** A link to a table row now brings the column names with it, the row you
landed on is marked, and a link inside highlighted Deluge no longer points at a function the report
has no card for. The band no longer floats over the content, which is what made anchors land behind
it - and the filter box is drawn only where it can work: opened from your workspace folder it
filters as before, opened straight from the panel it is absent rather than inert.

Nothing about what leaves your machine has changed.
