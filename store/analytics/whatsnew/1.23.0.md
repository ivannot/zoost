**Three more Zoho data centres.** Zoost now works on **zoho.sa**, **zoho.uk** and **zoho.ae** - until
now it was inert there: with no permission for those hosts the panel could not even tell it was
looking at Zoho Analytics. Because this adds hosts, Chrome may ask you to approve the update before
the extension runs again. Nothing else changed about what it reads.

**Go to Zoho Analytics goes to Zoho Analytics.** It used to open the workspace you had selected,
which is the wrong account the moment you sign out of one to sign into another. It opens your Zoho
Analytics home now, and beside it is a picklist of every data centre - because which account you want
next is your choice, not something the panel can deduce. Its starting point is a new **Data centre**
setting.

**Every finding in Health opens what it names.** «Nothing depends on them», «Tables in no relation»,
«Put there by Zoho», «Could not be read» - all of them were plain text, so the audit told you where
to look and then made you go and find it. Each row is a link now: it closes the audit and opens that
view, with its columns, its SQL and its lineage.

**Switching workspace resets the panel, not just the list.** With Health open, changing workspace
changed nothing on screen - what the switch rebuilt was the list underneath the overlay covering it.
A search term typed for one workspace also went on narrowing the next one.

**The diagram window shows the name you gave the workspace.** It only ever showed the one Zoho
Analytics derives - which is the whole reason that name exists, since the first workspace of every
account arrives called the same thing.

**Also:** Settings, Help and About stay reachable when there is no Zoho Analytics tab, and About no
longer opens underneath that screen; the workspace dropdown sorts by the name you actually see; the
explanation of **Max tool steps** sits next to the control it explains instead of next to the
passphrase; **Clear** in the chat is described for what it is, a cost control, since the whole
conversation is re-sent with every message; and a lapsed folder permission is reported in one
sentence that names the control which fixes it.
