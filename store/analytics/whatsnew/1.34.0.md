# Zoost Analytics 1.34.0

**A pull redraws what you have open.** The view you are reading is re-read and redrawn when the pull
finishes, instead of showing what the mirror held before it. A view the pull found deleted in Zoho no
longer sits in the pane as though it were still there.

**Open it on a Zoho CRM tab and it says so.** It used to refuse with "Not on a Zoho tab", which was
wrong twice over: the tab *was* Zoho, and the message did not say which product was wanted. It now
names the tab you are on, says that Zoost CRM reads it, and either tells you to click that
extension's icon in your toolbar or offers you its listing - whichever is true, because it asks the
other extension whether it is there. Inside Zoho One or Zoho CRM Plus, which can host either
product, it keeps the plain message rather than guess which one you meant.

**That screen is three groups instead of one list.** What to do when the tab belongs to the other
product, the pair that takes you to Zoho Analytics - the data-centre list exists only for the button
beside it - and the sample workspace at the foot. Each bordered in the colour of the product it is
about.

**A sample workspace is never shown as a live workspace.** Leaving a Zoho tab with the sample open
used to flip the workspace label to a real-looking binding, invented data included. It says what it
is.

Nothing about what leaves your machine has changed. The one message this version adds goes to the
other Zoost extension inside your own browser, is one word long, and never leaves the machine;
the privacy page on zoost.it describes it.
