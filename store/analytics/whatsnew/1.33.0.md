# Zoost Analytics 1.33.0

**Every control answers the keyboard.** Sixteen glyph buttons, the settings and About dialogs, the
assistant's controls and the diagram window's chips were clickable and nothing else; they are real
buttons now, Escape closes whatever is on top, and the focus ring is drawn where the browser left it
invisible.

**The file an export just wrote, one click away.** The report opens in a window of its own, and the
offer to open it lasts exactly as long as the line that put it there.

**The report you export.** The band no longer floats over the content, which is what made an anchor
land behind it: a jump lands what you asked for, below the band, without depending on anything the
browser might refuse to run. A chapter table's column names stay with you as you read down it. The
filter box is drawn only where it can work - opened from your workspace folder it filters as before,
opened straight from the panel it is absent rather than inert.

**The Workflows and Blueprints columns read as a list**, and the page has room for them.

Nothing about what leaves your machine has changed.
