# Zoost Analytics 2.0.2

Two fixes that came from the Zoho CRM side of the suite, where they were reported on a real org, and
that this panel had word for word. Nothing here changes what a pull reads or what lands on your disk.

**The panel no longer tells you there is nothing here when there is.** Opening Zoost with no Zoho
Analytics tab could cover the whole window with «Not on a Zoho Analytics tab» and offer two ways on -
go to Zoho Analytics, or write a sample workspace - over a workspace that was already mirrored. The
only thing actually missing was the folder permission, which Chrome drops between sessions. The panel
now says that, and one click anywhere restores it; the full-window message is kept for a panel that
really has nothing in it.

**The detail pane says it is reading.** Opening a view reads what it needs from disk, and until now
the pane went on showing the previous one while that happened: you clicked and nothing changed. There
is a spinner over the pane from the moment you click, and if a read takes more than two seconds it
says how long it has been waiting. It never says why: a slow read has several causes and naming the
wrong one would send you to fix something that is not broken.

**If opening an item is slow, the working folder is worth a look.** Measured on a real org: 1.9s to
open an item whose file a sync service was keeping in the cloud, 17ms for one already on the disk.
Nothing in Zoost can change the first of those - it is the file being fetched - and the guide now
says so, with the remedy. The guide also gained the warning about working folders inside OneDrive,
Dropbox and iCloud Drive that the Zoho CRM one has always carried.
