**Try everything without connecting anything.** `+ Sample` writes a workspace of invented data into
your working folder - views of every type, tables with their columns and foreign keys, query tables
with real SQL, an ER model, and a view nothing depends on so the audit has something to find. It
opens with no Zoho tab and no account: the list, the diagram, the columns, the lineage and the
exports all work on it. It is generated, never fetched, everything that would talk to Zoho Analytics
is refused for it and says so, and you delete it by deleting the folder.

**The diagram window is a different product.** The filters became two dimensions and multi-select,
and they rebuild the drawing instead of hiding pieces - so switching a kind off genuinely makes a
large workspace readable, and a view left with nothing attached is dropped rather than floated. The
focus belongs to the window now and applies to every view, instead of living inside the diagram. The
list folds away and the edge drags to resize it. The layout engine was rewritten: a large workspace
is drawn in a fraction of the time and shows its structure rather than a ring, and it says so while
it computes instead of freezing. Arrowheads keep their size as you zoom, and an arc attaches to the
side of the box that faces the other one. The Visual view is gone - it was a weaker drawing of what
the boxed diagram already shows.

**A pull no longer fails with a message from Chrome.** Chrome drops the folder permission between
sessions, and three of the five paths that write to disk went straight to it - so `Pull all`, `Pull`
and `Retry failed` could end in a sentence that named neither the folder nor the remedy. They ask
first now, and if the permission has lapsed the panel says which control fixes it, in the same words
on both Zoost extensions.

**An empty list names the reason it is empty** - *the* reason, not *a* reason. It used to recite the
whole sequence (pick a folder, create a workspace, pull) when the only thing in the way was one click
on **Grant access**. One click anywhere in the panel restores the folder permission, and the message
says so.

**Changing workspace drops what belonged to the old one**, the assistant's conversation included: a
reply naming another workspace's views sitting above a question about this one is worse than starting
again.

**Clear, in the chat, is a cost control.** The whole conversation is re-sent with every message, so a
long thread costs more per question and drags old context into new answers. The button says so, and
so does the assistant when you ask it.

**Also:** the settings page uses the width it has instead of a third of it; the icon was redrawn as a
single stroke, so the Z has one weight everywhere; the manifest declares its Content Security Policy
explicitly rather than relying on the default; and every piece of text you might copy out of the
extension uses plain ASCII punctuation, which survives being pasted anywhere.
