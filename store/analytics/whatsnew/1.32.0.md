**A workspace now opens on a view of itself.** The four-square button in the toolbar opens
**Workspace overview**: what is on disk for views, tables, relations and query SQL, when each was
last read, and what to do next. A workspace you have just created opens there by itself, with a
three-step path and one highlighted action; the sample starts at Browse, because its invented mirror
is already local.

**Getting started takes one click.** Pressing **Sample** when there is no working folder yet asks for
the folder, writes the sample and opens it, instead of stopping after the folder and leaving you to
find the next button. The same button then reads **Open sample** and takes you back to it.

**When Chrome drops the folder permission, the panel notices.** It always could lose it - a stored
folder handle does not survive a side panel being closed and reopened - and this side only worked it
out from one kind of read, so a refusal anywhere else left the panel believing it still had access:
the next click asked for nothing, and ↻ Refresh had to fail a read of its own before it would try
again. The folder is asked whenever any read is refused now, and **one click anywhere in the panel**
restores the access, which is what the message says.

**↻ Refresh no longer writes «Ready.» over its own warning.** A workspace with a file that will not
open says so - «N file(s) here would not open; what they hold is missing from every count below» -
and pressing Refresh replaced that sentence with «Ready.» while the panel showed the views with no
tables, no columns and no relations underneath.

**Switching to a workspace whose mirror is unusable no longer leaves the previous one on screen.** If
the last pull of the workspace you are switching to was interrupted, the bar above the list went on
naming the workspace you came from - offering to switch you to the one you were already in, with
Pull enabled underneath a bar saying pulling was off.

**An export that could not save your scope no longer disappears.** The tick boxes are remembered for
next time, and if the browser refused to store them the export stopped there: no file, no message,
nothing. It runs with what you ticked and says the default could not be remembered.

**Hovering a relation in the ER diagram now names it the way the arrow points.** It read the two
tables in the opposite order from the arrowhead and from the card that opens when you click the same
line.

**The assistant no longer sends you to Settings for export defaults**, which that page does not have -
the scope is chosen in the export dialog itself.

**The workspace overview button moved** out of the workspace bar and into the toolbar, beside Health,
AI and History - the controls it behaves like, and where the Zoho CRM panel keeps it.
