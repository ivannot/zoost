**Zoho One and Zoho CRM Plus.** Reaching Zoho Analytics through `one.zoho.<dc>` or `crmplus.zoho.<dc>` put it
inside a frame, and Zoost had nothing that could see into one: the panel opened and did nothing.
It now finds Zoho Analytics by asking each frame and keeping the one that answers - eighteen suite
addresses, and the plain `analytics.zoho.<dc>` behaves exactly as before.

**"Open in Zoho" no longer opens a second tab.** It moves the Zoho Analytics view you already
have, which is what the same control does in the other product.

**The HTML report is the twin of the Zoho CRM one.** It was the lesser of the two and now shares the same
frame: a heading band that stays with you, a filter box, a table of contents, links between the
sections - a report you can navigate rather than scroll - and the SQL is highlighted the way the
panel highlights it. It also carries an attribution line, which it had been missing entirely.

**Empty states say the one thing that is in the way**, in the same words the other product uses. A stored
folder loses its permission between sessions; a click anywhere in the panel restores it.

**The assistant** no longer answers with an empty message when the model spends its whole budget
thinking, and says when a reply was cut short.

Plus corrections across the diagrams, the SQL search after a pull, and both reports.
